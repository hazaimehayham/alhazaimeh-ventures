# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The site speaks to four audiences at once, so it has to read as credible to a banker and welcoming to a neighbor on the same page:

- **Investors and partners** evaluating whether Alhazaimeh Ventures is a serious, well-run group worth backing or doing business with.
- **Suppliers and brands** looking for distribution or partnership through the pharmacies and the fuel station.
- **Prospective talent and leadership** deciding whether this is a group worth joining.
- **Local customers and community members** getting to know the family name behind their pharmacies and station.

They arrive mostly cold, often on mobile, having heard the name or followed a link. They are deciding in the first few seconds whether this group is established and trustworthy. The job: leave understanding what the group operates, sensing it is real and durable, and knowing how to reach it.

## Product Purpose

Alhazaimeh Ventures is a diversified family holding group. It currently operates three pharmacies (Sadaf, Kahal, Edrak), a fuel station opening soon, and a proprietary healthcare-technology platform in private development. The site is the group's public face: it establishes credibility, presents the portfolio across healthcare, energy, and technology as one coherent house, and opens a direct line for partnership, supply, investment, and hiring inquiries.

Success is a visitor who leaves seeing an established, well-run group that is also building what comes next, and who knows exactly how to get in touch.

## Brand Personality

Established and trustworthy first: generational, dependable, the feel of a group that has been here and intends to stay. Refined and understated in execution (quiet confidence, careful craft, never flashy). Forward-looking enough that the healthcare-tech venture feels native to the brand, not bolted on. And warm at the root: family-run, community-serving, human.

The voice is plain and specific. It names real ventures and real places rather than reaching for adjectives. It is calm and assured, never salesy, never hyped. Three words: **established, refined, forward.**

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI template.** No cream/sand body backgrounds, no tiny uppercase tracked eyebrow above every section, no grid of identical icon-heading-text cards, no gradient accents or gradient text. The current build leans on several of these; they should be designed out, not preserved.
- **Loud corporate or salesy.** No buzzwords (streamline, empower, leverage, world-class, next-generation), no stock-photo handshakes, no aggressive CTAs. Credibility comes from specifics, not volume.
- **Cold tech startup.** Not navy-and-blue SaaS, not a dark hero with gradient blobs. This is a family holding group, not a software product.
- **Cluttered or busy.** No wall-to-wall sections, competing colors, or dense text blocks. Restraint reads as confidence.

## Design Principles

1. **Earn trust with specifics, not adjectives.** Name the pharmacies, the sectors, the real operations and numbers. Concrete detail is what makes "established" believable; generic praise undercuts it.
2. **One house, many ventures.** Pharmacy, fuel, and healthcare tech are very different businesses. The design must make them feel like deliberate facets of a single group, not a directory of unrelated logos.
3. **Heritage and horizon together.** Hold the generational, dependable feel and the forward-looking ambition in the same frame. Neither the legacy nor the tech venture should look like an afterthought.
4. **Warmth is human, not decorative.** Community-rootedness shows through tone, the people, and how the group talks about who it serves, not through warm-tinted backgrounds or soft styling for its own sake.
5. **Restraint over noise.** When in doubt, remove. Quiet, well-made, and clear beats loud and busy for every one of this site's audiences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 **AA** as the baseline: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast (watch the muted-gray-on-warm-background trap already present in the CSS), large text ≥3:1, full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every animation.

**Bilingual (Arabic / English)** is a first-class requirement given the regional audience: plan for right-to-left layout, logical CSS properties (inline/block, not left/right), Arabic-capable typography, and a language toggle. Treat RTL as a supported direction from the start, not a retrofit.
